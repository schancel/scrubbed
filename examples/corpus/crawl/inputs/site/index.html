Welcome & Overview

This fixture site exists only to prove the crawl example's discovery boundary and raw-output behavior. It links to two same-origin pages, each reachable in turn, and one deliberately out-of-scope external-looking page that a same-origin crawl must never fetch.

The station has operated continuously since the survey began, logging conditions across every season so the downstream sensor network keeps a complete, uninterrupted record for the whole watershed team to consult, well past the minimum length a short abstract page would carry.
