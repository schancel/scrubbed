Delta Survey Field Notes

The seven-person survey team spent three weeks mapping the tidal delta with a full research permit in hand, recording every transect in careful detail. Progress stayed steady even when the weather turned, and the crew stayed focused on finishing the western channel before the spring tides returned.

Instruments and method

Two sonar rigs and a hand-held current meter formed the core kit for the season, backed by a paper logbook that every team member signed at the end of each shift so nothing was lost between rotations.

Observations

  • Salinity rose sharply above the second channel bend, consistent with the dry-season baseline the team expected going in.
  • Sediment cores at station four showed three distinct depositional layers, more than any other station sampled this season.

The western channel behaves nothing like the model predicted, the lead hydrologist wrote in the shared logbook on the twelfth day of the survey.
